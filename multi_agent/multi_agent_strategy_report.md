1. **Champions/VIP** – protect high‑margin spend, turn advocacy into acquisition.

3. **At‑Risk** – recover ≥ 50 % of the **113,148 INR** at‑risk spend within 45 days via personalised offers and friction‑free returns.  
4. **New** – accelerate first‑order conversion; aim for **≥ 30 %** of new cohort to reach “Loyal” frequency within 90 days.

## 3. Discount & Fulfilment Fix Plan  

| Issue | Current Metric | Target / Action | Expected Impact |
|-------|----------------|----------------|-----------------|
| **High return/cancel rate (31.8 %)** | Return‑heavy categories: Home & Kitchen (34), Electronics (25), Fashion (25) | • **Quality gate** – add a “Fit‑Check” photo verification for Fashion & Home & Kitchen (pilot on 20 % of SKUs).<br>• **Return‑free incentive** – 10 % off + prepaid return label for the next purchase in the same category. | Reduce returns by **≈ 8 pp** (goal < 24 %). |
| **Rating drop on problem orders** | Cancelled = 2.54, Returned = 2.64 vs Delivered = 4.15 | • **Real‑time NPS trigger** – send a 1‑question SMS after a cancellation/return; route low scores (< 3) to a dedicated CX team.<br>• **Fulfilment SLA** – guarantee < 48 h dispatch for all “Champion” and “Loyal” orders; auto‑escalate delays. | Lift problem‑order rating to **≥ 3.2** within 60 days. |
| **West region revenue lag (115,430.30 INR)** | West = 14.7 % of total revenue | • **Regional discount tier** – introduce a **15 %** West‑only discount on Electronics (AOV 3,226.25 INR) for 4 weeks.<br>• **Logistics partnership** – add a local 2‑day hub to cut delivery time by 30 % (addresses rating dip).<br>• **Localized marketing** – push “Home & Kitchen” bundles (high return risk) with **free‑return** guarantee. | Target **+ 25 %** revenue uplift in West (≈ + 28,857 INR). |
| **Optimal discount level (AOV impact)** | AOV @0 % = 3,019.09 INR  <br>5 % = 3,464.33 INR  <br>10 % = 3,460.79 INR  <br>15 % = 3,226.25 INR  <br>20 % = 2,294.28 INR  <br>25 % = 2,010.35 INR | • **5 %–10 %** range yields the highest AOV (≈ 3,460 INR).<br>• Deploy **5 %** for “Loyal” bundle pushes; **10 %** for “At‑Risk” win‑back on favorite category. | Preserve basket size while stimulating conversion. |

**Implementation Timeline (30‑day sprints)**  

| Day 0‑7 | Day 8‑15 | Day 16‑30 |
